Help & Support

Frequently asked questions, application guidance, and official Maharashtra citizen helpline.

1. General Questions

Aaple Sarkar (आपले सरकार) is the official public service delivery portal initiated by the Government of Maharashtra under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act (RTS), 2015. It guarantees transparent, efficient, and time-bound delivery of essential citizen certificates and licenses without physical office visits.

Yes. All certificates downloaded through Aaple Sarkar carry a secure digital signature (e-Sign) from the designated competent authority along with a tamper-proof QR code. According to the IT Act 2000, they are 100% legally recognized across all institutions, colleges, banks, and courts in India without physical gazetted attestation.

2. Login and Registration

Click on Citizen Login in the header and toggle to New User Registration. Provide your full name, mobile number, district, and 12-digit Aadhaar number. A verification OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile to activate your account in under 2 minutes.

On the Login page, click the single "Forgot credentials?" link. You can reset your password or recover your username instantly by entering your registered 10-digit mobile number or Aadhaar number via an SMS OTP verification.

3. Application Process

Under the Right to Services (RTS) Act, every service has an enforceable SLA. For example, an Income Certificate is issued within 15 days, Domicile Certificate within 15 days, and Senior Citizen Certificate within 7 days. If a service is delayed, citizens can submit an online RTS First Appeal directly from their dashboard.

Once submitted, an application cannot be modified unless the scrutinizing department officer sends a "Query / Clarification Request". If an officer requests additional documentation, you will receive an SMS and a re-upload link will become active on your tracking page.

4. Document Requirements

Supporting documents can be uploaded in PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG format. Each document file size must be less than 2MB. Photograph size should be between 20KB and 500KB. Alternatively, you can use the Fetch from DigiLocker option to import verified documents directly with zero upload friction.

In most standard cases (income, domicile, farmer certificate), the process is completely faceless and digital. Only in complex land verification or contested caste scrutiny cases may a desk officer request an in-person meeting with original documents.

5. Certificate Download

You can download your certificate in two ways:
1. Go to the Track Application page, enter your Application ID, and click Download Digitally Signed Certificate.
2. Sign in to your citizen account and access your personal Citizen Locker tab where all historical certificates are saved forever.

Every generated certificate features a 2D QR Code and a 16-character Barcode. Anyone can scan the QR code with any smartphone camera to open the official Maharashtra Government verification portal showing authentic issue records.
